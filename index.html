What was checked

The starting point was JCR 2025: Education & Educational Research (764 journals) and Education, Scientific Disciplines (90 journals). Every SSCI or SCIE journal on mathematics, science, STEM or educational technology was checked for calls; journals on language, music, physical education, medicine and engineering were left out. ESCI journals were checked selectively, and journals whose sites block automated reading were checked by hand. A call counts as open only if its deadline falls on or after 23 Oct 2026.

Journal call pages checked, nothing open 14

Journal of Mathematics Teacher Education, Science & Education, JCAL, ijCSCL, Instructional Science, IJTDE, AJET (takes special-issue proposals each Aug–Sep), IJETHE, Journal of Computing in Higher Education, IRRODL, Journal of Baltic Science Education, IJLLS (special-issue proposals only), Mathematics Education Research Journal (ESCI), Journal of Computers in Education (ESCI).

The Asia-Pacific Education Researcher also has open collections on AI-mediated writing feedback (28 Feb 2027) and on SDG 4 (rolling); neither targets math or science. Journals whose calls appear in the lists above are not repeated here.

Second pass: general, teacher-education, policy and higher-education journals 116

Added on 24 Oct 2026. Educational psychology, special education, early childhood and subject journals outside math and science (language, arts, PE, medical) were left out.

About 35 were checked on an official page: Springer collection pages (Higher Education, Australian Educational Researcher, Journal of Educational Change, Educational Assessment Evaluation and Accountability, Research in Higher Education, Minerva, Studies in Philosophy and Education, Higher Education Policy), SAGE’s list of open calls (covering its education journals, including Journal of Teacher Education), AERA’s journal page (AERJ, AERA Open, Educational Researcher, EEPA, RER, RRE), Emerald’s call list, and the RIED, RELIME and Elementary School Journal sites. About 20 more were searched by name. The remaining 60 or so, mostly Taylor & Francis, Wiley and Elsevier titles plus several non-English journals, could not be checked one by one because those sites block automated reading.

Taylor & Francis was then covered in full through its own list of current education calls (94 distinct calls across Taylor & Francis, Routledge and Cogent, collected by hand on 24 Oct 2026). Matched against JCR 2025: 34 calls are in SSCI journals, 32 in ESCI journals and 28 in journals outside both lists. Calls on language, physical education, early childhood, educational psychology and other off-topic themes were left out.

The 16 remaining Wiley and Elsevier titles were then checked by hand on their call pages (International Journal of Educational Research, International Journal of Educational Development, Educational Research Review, The Internet and Higher Education, Studies in Educational Evaluation, Learning, Culture and Social Interaction, Economics of Education Review, British Educational Research Journal, European Journal of Education, Educational Measurement: Issues and Practice, Mind, Brain, and Education, Anthropology & Education Quarterly, Science Education, JRST, Journal of Engineering Education, Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education). Three open calls turned up: Studies in Educational Evaluation and Journal of Engineering Education (listed above) and European Journal of Education (listed under university focus).

New open calls from this pass: Distance Education, RIED, JRTE and Educational Philosophy and Theory (listed above), plus IJMEST’s automated-assessment issue and three Cogent Education collections (listed under ESCI). Journal of Teacher Education’s Black Teacher Project issue is past its abstract stage; Journal of Educational Change and Australian Educational Researcher have only open-ended collections.
